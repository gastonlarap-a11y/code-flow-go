The diagram is a workflow (`diagram_type: "workflow"`): one process, step by step, with who does each
step.
- Lanes are who acts — a user, a service, a job, a team — two to five of them; put failures, retries
  and refusals in a lane of their own.
- Nodes are the steps, six to sixteen of them. Each sits in a lane and a column; the column is the
  step's order along the main path, and steps that happen at the same time share one.
- Edges say how the process moves: the happy path, the alternatives, what runs in the background,
  what goes back, what fails. List the happy path's node ids, in order, as the main path.
- Label an edge that leaves a decision with the condition that takes it.
